Tumble

Tumble is an 8×8 block puzzle with gravity. Clear a row or column and the blocks above fall into the gap; if they complete another line, it clears too. There's also a daily mode where everyone gets the same pieces, with a Game Center leaderboard.

Support

Questions, bug reports, or ideas: open an issue at github.com/aibrahim11/tumble/issues.

  • Purchases: if Remove ads didn't take effect, open Settings inside Tumble and tap Restore purchases.
  • Leaderboards: to appear on them, sign in to Game Center in the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
  • Daily mode: a new Daily starts at 00:00 UTC, which is 8 p.m. Eastern during daylight saving time and 7 p.m. Eastern the rest of the year. Everyone gets the same pieces.
