Tumble

A block puzzle with gravity. Drag pieces onto the 8×8 board, clear rows and columns, and watch the blocks above tumble down into new lines. Chains, colour bonuses, and a daily challenge with a global leaderboard.

Support

Questions, bug reports, or ideas: open an issue on GitHub. Every report is read.

  • Purchases: use Settings → Restore purchases in the app if Remove Ads did not apply.
  • Leaderboards: sign in to Game Center in iOS Settings to appear on the boards.
  • Daily challenge: resets at 00:00 UTC. Everyone gets the same pieces.
